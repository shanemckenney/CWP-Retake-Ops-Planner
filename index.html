Score Report Intake

What this is: your own personal retake study plan, built from your actual score report. It only takes a couple minutes to set up.
  1. Enter your name, exam, score, and the date you're planning to retake.
  2. On the next screen, you'll paste or check off the specific objectives you missed.
  3. You'll get a day-by-day plan up until your retake — it saves automatically on this device, so you can close the tab and come back to it tomorrow.
This is meant for you to fill out yourself, on your own device — it won't work right if someone else's plan gets passed around on a shared file.

Step 2 / 3

Missed Objectives

Look at your CompTIA score report — it lists the specific objective numbers (like 2.1, 3.4) you missed, usually under a "performance by objective" section.
  1. Copy those numbers and paste them into the box below, then click "Match objectives."
  2. Anything it can't confidently match will show up for you to check off manually underneath — that's normal, not an error.
  3. Don't have the report handy, or numbers weren't listed? Just check off what you remember missing in the list below instead.
